PetCare 360 Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

App version covered: PetCare 360 1.2.0

PetCare 360 (“PetCare 360,” “we,” or “us”) is a pet-care record-keeping application. This policy explains what information the current version of the app uses and how it is handled.

1. Information you enter or add

Depending on the features you use, information may include pet names, species, breed, dates of birth, photographs, microchip numbers, allergies, medical conditions, medications, medication logs, vaccination and veterinary records, appointments, health observations, feeding and care schedules, expenses, insurance details, caregiver names and contact details, notes, and documents or receipts you attach. Some of this information may be sensitive. Please enter only information you are comfortable storing on your device.

2. Where information is stored

In version 1.2.0, PetCare 360 stores app records locally on the iPhone using Apple's on-device persistence technology. This version does not implement PetCare 360 cloud hosting, CloudKit household synchronization, or cross-device family sharing. Do not rely on the app to synchronize records to another person's device.

Apple may include app data in device backups according to the iOS and iCloud backup settings you control. Those backups are governed by Apple's services and settings.

The app can create a JSON backup file when you choose Export. The file is shared or saved only through the iOS share interface you select. You are responsible for keeping exported backup files secure. Importing a backup stores its contents in the app on your device.

3. Photos, camera and document scanning

If you choose to add a pet photo, receipt, or medical document, the app accesses the photo or camera functionality you select. Document text recognition uses Apple Vision on the device. The current implementation does not send scanned images to a PetCare 360 server for OCR. Original documents and photos are stored with app data on the device.

4. Emergency QR codes and sharing

The emergency QR feature creates a code on your device using only the fields you enable. The QR code contains the selected information in readable form; it is not an encrypted online profile. Anyone who obtains and scans a copy of the QR code may be able to read the information encoded in it. Share it carefully. You can turn the feature off or change the selected fields in the app.

When you choose to share a PDF, QR image, or backup, the content is passed to the iOS share interface and the destination you choose. The recipient and destination service then handle it under their own terms and privacy policies.

5. Notifications

Reminders are scheduled as local notifications on your device. If you grant notification permission, iOS may display reminder information on your device according to your notification settings. The current version does not require a PetCare 360 notification server.

6. Purchases and subscriptions

In-app subscriptions are processed by Apple through StoreKit and your Apple Account. PetCare 360 does not receive or store your full payment card details. Apple handles payment processing, billing, refunds and subscription management under Apple's terms and privacy policy. The app receives transaction and entitlement information needed to determine whether Premium features are available.

7. Analytics, advertising and tracking

The current version does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs and does not use the app to track you across other companies' apps or websites. The app does not operate a PetCare 360 account or application backend in version 1.2.0.

8. Third-party services and links

The app may link to external resources, including official FDA animal-food recall information and Apple services. When you open an external link or use an Apple service, that provider's privacy terms apply. We do not control third-party websites.

9. Retention and deletion

Your app records remain on your device until you delete them or remove the app, subject to any device backups you have enabled. Use the in-app Delete all my data function to remove the app's stored records. Separately exported files and device backups are not deleted by that in-app action; you must manage those copies yourself through the locations where you saved them.

10. Children

PetCare 360 is intended for adults responsible for the care of household pets. It is not designed to collect personal information directly from children.

11. Security

We rely on Apple's platform protections and the security of your device. No method of electronic storage or transmission is guaranteed to be completely secure. Use a device passcode and keep your device and operating system updated.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's features or practices change. The updated policy will be published at this URL with a revised effective date. Material changes should be reviewed before you continue using affected features.
